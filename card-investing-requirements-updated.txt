CARD INVESTING — REQUIREMENTS

1. Card Inventory
- Maintain a central record for every card.
- Track card details, purchase information, grading information, sale information, profit, and ROI/percentage.
- Support filtering, sorting, searching, and different views of the card data.
- Card status should reflect its current lifecycle stage and may be derived from multiple factors rather than always entered manually.

2. Card Lifecycle
- Track cards from purchase through their lifecycle, including stages such as in transit, received, grading/submission, returned, listed, and sold.
- Cards can share/inherit a grouped status while being prepared for grading and while they are part of an active PSA submission.
- Once grading is completed, each card's status must be independently and manually controllable because post-grading workflows can vary greatly from card to card.
- A completed PSA submission remains part of the card's history but no longer controls the card's current status.
- Detailed status rules will be defined later.

3. PSA Submissions
- PSA submissions are separate records from cards.
- Allow multiple cards to be selected and added to a PSA submission.
- A submission can contain many cards.
- Submission-level status/stage changes should automatically be reflected by all cards belonging to that submission.
- Individual grading results remain associated with each card.

4. PSA Grading / Gem Rate
- Provide a separate view/table for completed PSA submissions.
- Automatically calculate grading statistics from the cards in each completed submission.
- Statistics should include total cards, PSA 10, PSA 9, PSA 8.5, PSA 8, PSA 7 or lower, no grade, Gem Rate, and percentage graded PSA 9 or better.
- Support grading analytics across submissions and, eventually, other dimensions such as player, set, year, and category.

5. Personal Collection
- Track personal-collection cards separately from investment inventory in the UI while using the same underlying card format/data model.
- Collection views can expose a different subset of card fields appropriate for collection tracking.
- Track collection cost separately from investment totals.

6. Moving Cards Between Investment and Collection
- Allow cards to move from Investment to Personal Collection and back without creating duplicate card records.
- Moving an investment card to the collection removes its cost from investment totals and adds it to collection costs.
- Preserve the card's original purchase/cost information when it moves.
- If a collection card later moves back to investments and is sold, calculate profit/ROI using its original cost basis.
- Preserve enough history to know that a card moved between investment and collection if needed.

7. Financial Tracking
- Track purchase/cost basis for investment cards.
- Track sale price/date and calculate profit and percentage/ROI.
- Investment totals should include only cards currently considered investments as appropriate to the reporting rules.
- Collection costs should be tracked separately from investment totals.

8. Data Entry / Autocomplete
- Speed up card entry by suggesting values from previously entered records.
- For example, after "Lionel Messi" has been entered once, typing part of the name on a future card should offer "Lionel Messi" as an autocomplete option.
- Autocomplete may also be used for other repeatable fields such as set, category, purchased from, and eBay seller where appropriate.
- Users must still be able to enter new values that do not already exist.

9. Reporting / Views
- Provide separate views for investment cards, personal collection, PSA submissions, and completed-submission grading/Gem Rate results.
- Eventually support dashboards, filters, and graphs based on the same underlying data.
- Avoid maintaining duplicate information across separate tables/files when values can be derived from existing card or submission data.

10. Existing Numbers Data
- Existing Numbers files will provide the initial data for cards, PSA submissions/Gem Rate history, and personal collection.
- The goal is to consolidate these into one connected system rather than continuing to maintain separate Numbers files.

This is a working requirements document and should be revised as workflows and rules are defined.
